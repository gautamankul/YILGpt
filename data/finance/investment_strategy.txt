Title: Investment Strategy Guidelines

Objective:
To maximize long-term returns while minimizing risk.

Asset Allocation:
- 60% equities
- 30% fixed income
- 10% alternative investments

Key Concepts:
- Diversification reduces risk
- Compounding increases returns over time

Performance Metrics:
- ROI (Return on Investment)
- Sharpe Ratio
- Alpha and Beta

Review Cycle:
Portfolio should be reviewed quarterly.
